# SkillOps: owner's review, 10 Nov 2026

Each part of SkillOps was looked at as Robomy's owner would: does it hold up Robomy's name, does it work,
does it look and feel good? Then: what should change, what is missing, what is not needed, and what is
logically wrong. The last column says what was done about it in this round.

## 2. Overall analysis

**Look and feel.** The app is consistent and calm, but the trainer's main screen (the runbook) was the
weakest. Teaching happens in front of a class, so the screen must be read in a glance from a distance:
whole sections, large type, nothing to scroll down through. The class view does this.

**Missing.** A record of what was taught (class recap), a way to say "this needs another pass" (repeat
flag), and recognition of delivery (milestones, trainer activity). These are what turn SkillOps from a
checklist into something that drives better delivery and visible results.

**Not needed.** The duplicate "Overview" entries in the long runbook tree (the class view replaces the tree
for teaching); MCQ and Live data as separate top-level tabs next to teaching (now one Tools hub); the
"Programmes" page and the institution page overlap but both are useful, so both stay.

**Logical flaws.** "Continue" went to the first gap instead of the class's real position (fixed). Ticking
reloaded the page and lost the reader's place (fixed in the class view). The MCQ "tests written" figure
counted attempts (renamed).

## 3. What stays the same on purpose

No schedule language and no league tables: recognition shows what each trainer delivered, never who is
"ahead". All numbers still come straight from stored ticks.

## 4. Second round: the eleven problems found in use

| # | Problem | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The supervisor could mark items done | Marking, un-marking, notes and repeat flags are the trainers' work: the server refuses them for the supervisor (403), and the class view, detail view and batch page show **view only** / **View** instead of the buttons. Trainer routes were swept again: every batch page and every write checks the batch; live-data and MCQ writes also check the student, code or test belongs to it. |
| 2 | No way to add data; the tool's name | **Live Data Simulator** everywhere (menus, pages, API `platform`, template default). **Add data** uploads a .db or .csv, reads its tables, and loads them with the supervisor's choices (background jobs; the Jobs Lambda got 4 GB of /tmp). |
| 3 | Archived items hard to tell apart | Archived rows are filled red with an **ARCHIVED** badge; archived pages carry a red banner; turned-off trainers look the same. Still nothing is deleted. |
| 4 | No way back | A trail at the top of every page below the menu (*Batches › Batch A › …*): every level above is one click away. |
| 5 | Trainers mixed across institutes | Trainers have a home institution; the Trainers page is grouped by institution with a filter, and each institution page lists its trainers. |
| 6 | Old live-data pages did not fit | The public landing and status pages are gone (redirects kept); **Register** is a plain server form and the **Developer docs** use the SkillOps look. Their old scripts and stylesheet are removed. |
| 7 | Uploaded runbooks could not be deleted | An upload (version) or a whole runbook can be deleted when nothing uses it: no project, no batch on it, no ticks on it. Ticked versions stay as history. |
| 8 | Session planning | **Session planner**: the runbook page by page on the left, the plan on the right; drag parts in, reorder, add own lines and a note, for any date. One plan per batch per date, shared by its trainers; the supervisor reads only. Today's plan shows on the batch page and in the class view. |
| 9 | Trainers could not compare | **Team progress**: every batch in the trainer's programmes side by side, "You" against the average of the others. Listed by name; other batches cannot be opened. |
| 10 | Mapping was hard | Four steps shown at the top. "We found 10 Steps · 34 Parts · 418 Actions" with one **Looks right** button (which also remembers the layout); patterns drawn as they look in Word, level pills with live counts. |
| 11 | Class days per batch | **Class days** per batch (supervisor): weekdays, first/last day, days off, extra days. Trainers see a calendar of class days, with planned and not-yet-planned days, and plan each one. |

## 5. Third round

- The trail replaces Back; Delete sits under every Archive (with the name typed to confirm); datasets and feeds of
  the Live Data Simulator can be deleted; closed or expired join codes disappear.
- Uploading a large .db on the laptop works (up to 450 MB through the page; in AWS the browser sends it straight
  to S3). Signed-in staff are sent away from the students' Register page.
- Team progress compares only within the trainer's programme (one institute).
- The batch page leads with the current project in detail (the supervisor can choose it), the class-day calendar,
  **Teach** (today's planned session only) and **View runbook**; other projects are folded away.
- The runbook reader and the planner open in their own large window, with a section pane that folds away and
  Previous / Next under each section.
