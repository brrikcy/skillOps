# SkillOps: trainer guide (one page)

**Sign in.** Open the SkillOps address your supervisor sent you. Type your email and password.
The first time, use the temporary password from the email "Your SkillOps account", then choose your own
(at least 10 characters, with a capital letter, a small letter and a number). Forgot it? Press
**Forgot password?** and use the code from the email.

**Your batches.** After sign-in you open straight into your first batch. The **Batches** bar just under the
SkillOps title has one tab per batch: click a name to switch. **Team progress** is at the right end of the bar.
(The bar scrolls sideways on a phone if you have many batches.) The line at the top of a page (for example
*Batch A › Live Data Simulator*) takes you to any level above in one click.

**A batch.** Each batch has four tabs: **Progress**, **Today**, **Calendar** and **Tools** (Live Data
Simulator and MCQ tests). A batch always opens on **Progress**.

**Progress** fits on one screen:
- Five figures across the top: the **programme** done, the **current project** done, items **marked done in
  the last 7 days**, items that **need a repeat**, and **gaps** (items still open before the furthest one done).
- **Where the class is**: the next item of the current project (chosen by your supervisor) and when the last
  item was marked. **Projects** below it: one bar per project; the current one is in colour. Click a name to
  read that runbook; **CSV** downloads the figures. Projects without a runbook yet are folded into one line.
- The **steps** of the current project, each with a bar. Click a step to open its parts (one step opens at a
  time); click a part to teach it in the class view.
- **Items marked done per day** for the last 4 weeks; the shaded band is the last 7 days. Point at (or tab to)
  a column to see the day and the number.
- **Recent activity**, **Needs a repeat**, **Gaps** and **Trainer notes** are folded; open one at a time.
On a big screen, a long list opens inside its own box, so the page itself never needs scrolling.

**Today.** Today's session: the plan with **Teach** and **Change the plan**, or **Plan today's session** if there
is none. **View runbook** opens the whole runbook in its own large window, as the original PDF, scrolling
down like a document. The sections are on the left (hide them with «): press one, or a part inside it, and the
PDF jumps there; type in **Find a section or part** to narrow the list, **Fold all** / **Open step** tidy it, and
the round button beside an action marks it done. **Open PDF** and **Download** are at the top. **Coming class days** lists the next class days
with **Plan** or **Open**; **Covered today** shows what was marked done today, with the **Recap**. The
**Calendar** tab shows the whole month.

**Teach.** **Teach** opens *today's planned session*: only the parts you planned, in your order, each with
**Mark done**.

**Reading the runbook in class.** Open a step on **Progress** and click a part: the runbook opens in its large
window, in the original PDF, at that part (the same window as **View runbook**). A runbook whose PDF your
supervisor has not attached yet still opens as pages in columns, like a newspaper (read down a column, then move
right with **→ / Page Down**). Planning a session always uses the runbook's own pages beside the timeline.

**Mark done.** Press **Mark done** on an item when the class has done it. It saves in place ("Saved"); if the
Wi-Fi drops it says "Not saved, retrying" and keeps trying. A double click never makes two ticks. You can
un-mark your own ticks. Each batch keeps its own ticks. Press **↻** on an item when the class needs it
again: it is flagged **needs a repeat** for this batch and listed on **Progress**. Add a longer note in
the Detail view; your supervisor sees it.

**Plan a session.** Press **Plan** next to a class day (**Today** tab or calendar). It opens in its own large
window: the sections on the left (hide them with «), one section of the runbook in the middle with **Previous** /
**Next** below, and the **Session planner** on the right. Choose the date at the top. Drag any action (or a whole section with **Whole section**) into the planner, in the order you will
teach it, and drag the entries to reorder them. **+** adds without dragging. Add your own lines ("Break",
"Quiz on joins") and a note. Nothing is saved until you press **Save plan**. The batch's trainers share one plan per date; the
**Today** tab shows it, and **Teach** opens it. **Download PDF** gives the saved plan as a PDF to print or keep:
a cover that lists the session, then every planned part in your order. When your supervisor has attached the
runbook's PDF, each part is cut from it and looks exactly as in the runbook; otherwise it is laid out from the
runbook's text, code and tables. A whole section comes with its introduction and every action in it. (If you have
unsaved changes it asks whether to save first.)

**See how the team is doing.** **Team progress** (right end of the Batches bar) shows the batches of your programme at your
institute side by side: what each is teaching now, how much of the programme is done, and items delivered in the last 7 days.
Your batches and your own figures are marked **You**, next to the average of the other trainers there. You can see other
batches here but not open them.

**Send the class recap.** After class open **Recap** (**Today** tab, **Covered today**). It lists what was
covered today and where the next class starts. Press **Copy** and paste it into the class group or an email.

**Make a join code (Live Data Simulator).** Batch → **Tools** → **Live Data Simulator** → choose how many students and for how long →
**Make a join code**. Show the code to the class. Students register on their own device at the address shown under the code
(`…/register`; signed-in staff are sent back to SkillOps from that page). Students read the Developer docs at `…/docs`. A closed or expired code disappears. If you ticked "I approve each student", approve them
in the Students list. **New key** gives a student a new API key (shown once). The activity table shows who
has fetched data.

**Open an MCQ test and watch it.** Batch → **Tools** → **MCQ tests**. You can open and close the tests made
for your batch; tests shared by all batches are opened by your supervisor. Press **Open** on a test (and **Close** when the time
is over). **Copy link** gives the student link. **Monitor** shows each student live (refreshes every 10
seconds): answered, warnings, time left; **+5 min** adds time; **End test** ends one student's test.
**Results** and **Marks register** have a CSV button. **Import questions** takes a CSV file with the headings
Question, A, B, C, D, Answer, Marks. If the test system does not answer, the page says so; everything else
in SkillOps keeps working.

**Light or dark.** The moon / sun button at the top switches SkillOps between light and dark; it remembers your
choice on that computer. Until you choose, it follows your computer's own setting. Runbook pages stay light, like
paper, so the runbook's coloured boxes read the same.

**If something goes wrong** the page says what happened and what to do, with a request ID. Send the ID to
your supervisor. If your sign-in ends while you are saving something, sign in again and press
**Save it now**.
